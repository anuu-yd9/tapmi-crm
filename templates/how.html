How enquiries work behind the scenes

Every enquiry flows through one CRM that captures it, scores it, follows up automatically and keeps working after the sale. This page explains the logic.

The flow

  1. Capture

    Each buyer situation has its own form. The link a visitor came from is recorded as the lead source.

  2. Qualify

    A score out of 100 sorts the lead into Hot, Warm or Cold using the questions on the form.

  3. Follow up

    A confirmation email goes out at once, then reminders and nurture emails follow a plan matched to the score.

  4. Convert

    Counsellors move the lead through its pipeline. Each stage creates the next task.

  5. Retain and develop

    After enrolment: check-ins, repeat cohorts, credit stacking and referral requests.

Hot 70 and above

Qualified as a sales lead at once. A person calls within 24 hours.

Warm 40 to 69

Marketing-qualified. Nurture emails plus a reminder to book a call.

Cold below 40

Slow, low-touch nurture, with a reminder to re-score after 30 days.

Where the AI helps, and where people decide

  • The AI summarises each lead, suggests a priority and the next action, and drafts a personal follow-up email.
  • It is only given the programme facts and fees held in this system, and is told never to promise admission, placement, salary, rankings or accreditation.
  • It never sends anything. A counsellor reads, edits and approves each draft.
  • People stay in charge of pricing exceptions, proposals, and every conversation about money.
