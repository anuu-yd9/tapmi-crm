ABOUT TAPMI BENGALURU

Four decades of management education, now in Bengaluru.

TAPMI Bengaluru is a constituent unit of Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE), and extends the legacy of T. A. Pai Management Institute to Bengaluru.

Its executive education pathways are designed for professionals who want to broaden their leadership capabilities and organisations that want to build practical, workplace-ready skills.

Learning begins with your goals: understanding the business challenge or career ambition, then shaping an experience that can be applied at work.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

A few helpful answers as you explore the executive education pathways.

What executive education pathways are featured?

This website presents three pathways: the Executive MBA, Management Development Programme and Management Certifications.

Which programme is suitable for an organisation?

Organisations can explore the Executive MBA for sponsored professionals, the Management Development Programme for a customised manager cohort, and Management Certifications for focused employee upskilling.

Can working professionals study alongside their jobs?

The programme information describes weekend and blended learning formats. Review the individual programme page for its stated structure and schedule.

How do I find the programme details and posters?

Visit the Brochures section to view the programme posters, or open an individual programme page for its summary, duration, format and fee information.

Is this an official TAPMI admissions website?

No. This is a student project and is not an official TAPMI offering or admissions website. Programme information and fees should be independently confirmed with TAPMI.
